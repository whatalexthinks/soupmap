Marugame, west of Takamatsu on the Kagawa coast, is a town of udon shops in the heart of Sanuki udon country: firm, square-cut noodles in a light iriko (dried sardine) dashi, served from early morning to early afternoon, often self-service. Many visitors know the name from Marugame Seimen, the nationwide udon chain, but the chain was founded in Kakogawa, in Hyogo Prefecture; the name is a tribute to the town, not a sign of where it started. The udon in Marugame itself is mostly small, local shops.

The town has two other draws. Marugame Castle keeps one of the twelve original castle keeps surviving in Japan, small but perched high on stone walls. And the town is known for honetsuki-dori, a whole chicken leg roasted on the bone with pepper and garlic, often eaten with beer in the evening, after the udon shops have closed. The Kotohira-gu shrine (Konpira-san) is a short train ride inland. Read [Sanuki udon: a whole prefecture built on a bite](/journal/sanuki-udon/) and see [Udon in Takamatsu](/guides/udon-in-takamatsu/) for the capital.

## Know before you go
- Udon is a morning and lunch food here; plan dinner around honetsuki-dori instead.
- Self-service shops expect you to clear your own tray.
