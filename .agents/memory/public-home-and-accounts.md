---
name: Public homepage and optional accounts
description: Product boundaries between marketing, public map exploration and private account lists.
---
Soupmap needs a homepage that explains the product and encourages signup, while visitors can enter the map at any time without an account.

**Why:** The user explicitly said “there should be a home page that explains everything and also aims at getting people to sign up but also can enter the map anytime to view.”

**How to apply:** Never turn public map browsing or shared-list viewing into a mandatory signup flow. Accounts are for private personal lists and cross-device saves, not permission to browse.

Keep the public homepage reachable even after sign-in, and provide clear navigation between home, map, content and accounts on mobile as well as desktop.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to get back to the main website page or elsewhere while clicking around the application and map.

**How to apply:** Do not auto-redirect signed-in homepage visitors away to their lists. Personal lists remain a separate navigation destination.

Existing device saves must remain independent until the user explicitly imports them into their account; importing adds rather than replaces saves. Signing up does not imply consent to marketing emails.

**Why:** Account adoption must not lose existing guest choices, merge a shared device's private choices silently, or turn authentication into an undisclosed mailing-list subscription.

**How to apply:** Keep guest storage separate from signed-in lists, offer an explicit non-destructive import, and obtain separate consent before any future marketing subscription.

New personal saves require an account. Prompt at save actions with contextual benefits and email signup; do not create new device-only bookmarks, trips, passport memories or private recipe notes.

**Why:** The user explicitly changed the saving policy to account/email signup everywhere appropriate, with persuasive sales messaging.

**How to apply:** Keep public browsing, planning and cooking open. Explain cross-device/private saving, offer sign-in and a clear dismissal, and never treat a failed account request as permission to write locally.
