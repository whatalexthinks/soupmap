Use Japanese minimalism and brutalism together for the homepage: different typography from the previous italic-serif travel-magazine treatment and clearer information layouts.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to “think japanese minimalism and brutalist at the same time” and said the fonts and information layouts should be different. They did not reject the warm colors, but were open to improving them.

**How to apply:** Keep the page restrained and structured with crisp typography and useful product information, rather than returning to decorative travel-magazine slogans and serif flourishes.

Show the genuine app experience in a phone frame instead of generated maps or fictional street photos.

**Why:** The user said the generated paper-map trail and alley image were “way too ai” and supplied an actual Soupmap screenshot as the preferred visual reference. They did not reject the ramen mood image.

**How to apply:** Prefer actual app screenshots for product demonstrations. Do not reintroduce the rejected generated map/alley images or invent a replacement map UI. Distinguish any remaining generated food imagery from actual shop photography.
