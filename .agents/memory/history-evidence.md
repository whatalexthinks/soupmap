---
name: History evidence policy
description: Source and rights boundaries for the opt-in history layer.
---

Historical context must not be inferred from photorealistic tiles or assigned to
a shop merely because a historical place is nearby. PLATEAU physical attributes
remain separate from narratives.

**Why:** The historical-context request explicitly requires sourced dates,
unknown facts and separate building facts. Religious origin traditions and
unreferenced structured inception statements can differ without either being a
surviving building's construction date.

**How to apply:** Keep each date's meaning and uncertainty visible. For example,
Sensoji's 628 sacred-image tradition and Wikidata's unreferenced 645 inception
must not be silently collapsed into a construction year.

Use independently written summaries of facts from official sources, and
explicitly attributed, share-alike adaptations where Wikipedia text is used.
Archive metadata does not license underlying scans, thumbnails or book text.

**Why:** A source's availability is not permission to reuse its expression or
images; Japan Search aggregates items with different rights.

**How to apply:** Check rights per reused work, preserve revision/source links
and modification notices, and do not add historical images without individual
license checks. Consult `docs/history-context.md` for the maintenance policy.
