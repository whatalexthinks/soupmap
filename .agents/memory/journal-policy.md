---
name: Journal content policy
description: Voice and truthfulness rules for the Soupmap Journal articles.
---

The user asked for "structured blogs about Japan, ramen and soups of all kinds, history,
relevant information that is factual and written in an interesting personal blog type of way",
in a Japanese-minimalist design.

**Why:** The journal is the main organic-search surface and must be trustworthy.

**How to apply:** Follow `content/journal/README.md`. Personal, opinionated voice is welcome;
invented experiences, named friends or quotes are not. Hedge origin legends, avoid statistics,
prices and hours, and keep the build validator and `tests/journal.test.ts` green.
